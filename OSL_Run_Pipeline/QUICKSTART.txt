================================================================================
REQUIREMENTS FOR GOOD ACCURACY
================================================================================

1. Lighting
   - Bright, even lighting on hands and face
   - Avoid backlighting and harsh shadows
   - Natural daylight or LED preferred

2. Camera position
   - Webcam at eye level
   - Entire body in frame (or at least torso and hands)
   - Camera 1-2 meters away

3. Hand movements
   - Clear, deliberate hand motions
   - Complete the sign fully (~1-2 seconds)
   - Avoid rapid jerky movements
   - Let hands stabilize at end of sign

4. Background
   - Plain or simple background preferred
   - No clutter behind signer

5. Clothing
   - Avoid loose sleeves that hide hands
   - Neutral colored clothing for contrast

================================================================================
WHAT THE SCRIPT DOES
================================================================================

1. Loads model
   - Reads trained checkpoint from disk
   - Initializes Uni-Sign ISLR model
   - Moves to GPU (or CPU)
   - Sets to evaluation mode

2. Initializes pose extraction
   - Sets up MediaPipe Holistic
   - Prepares for real-time keypoint extraction

3. Opens webcam
   - Captures frames at 30 FPS
   - Displays webcam feed with pose skeleton

4. Records pose sequences
   - When SPACE pressed: captures pose keypoints
   - Maintains buffer of last 64 frames
   - Shows frame count in real-time

5. Runs inference
   - When R pressed: runs model on recorded sequence
   - Extracts top-5 predictions
   - Shows confidence scores
   - Displays on screen

6. Visualizations
   - Shows live webcam feed
   - Overlays pose skeleton (MediaPipe)
   - Displays status and instructions
   - Shows last prediction

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING - QUICK FIXES
================================================================================

Problem: Module import errors
Fix: cd to script directory and ensure PYTHONPATH includes Uni-Sign

Problem: Webcam not opening
Fix: Check if another app uses webcam, unplug/replug, restart

Problem: Out of memory
Fix: Close other apps or use --device cpu

Problem: No pose landmarks detected
Fix: Better lighting, ensure hands visible, get closer to camera

Problem: Low accuracy
Fix: Slow down sign movements, ensure good lighting, train longer

Problem: Script crashes on startup
Fix: Check all required files exist (checkpoint, labels file)

See README_INFERENCE.md for detailed troubleshooting

================================================================================
PERFORMANCE TIPS
================================================================================

For better accuracy:
- Record at least 30 frames (~1 second) per sign
- Use good lighting
- Avoid rapid jerky movements
- Keep entire body in frame

For faster computation:
- Use GPU instead of CPU
- Reduce WEBCAM_WIDTH/HEIGHT to 480p

For more stable predictions:
- Increase MIN_FRAMES_TO_RECOGNIZE
- Increase SMOOTHING_WINDOW in Config
